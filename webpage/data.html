    Survey data is only available to CivicBuddy surveyors. Submit at least one survey in the app with this Google account, then come back. If you use a different Google account in the app, sign out and sign in with that one.

    What's inside the ZIP?
    • surveys.csv: one row per survey (ID, start/end time, counts, surveyor ID).
    • points.csv: one row per survey point: GPS, accuracy, area, time, photo files and every surveyor question with its answer.
    • citizen_interviews.csv: one row per interview: occupation, gender, age, location and every citizen question with its answer.
    • surveys/<survey ID>/: the full survey as survey.json and its photos in point_1/photo_1.jpg and so on.
    • all_surveys.json: all data exactly as stored, for programmers.

    CSV files open in Excel and Google Sheets, Bengali text included.
