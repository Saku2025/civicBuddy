How it works

From street corner to survey report in four steps

  1. 1

    Sign in with Google

    One tap and you're ready. Your surveys are linked to your account and private to you.

  2. 2

    Add a survey point

    CivicBuddy records your GPS position, timestamp and area name automatically.

  3. 3

    Capture & observe

    Take up to 5 photos per point and answer the surveyor questionnaire on cleanliness, waste type, odour and more.

  4. 4

    Interview & submit

    Record a citizen interview, then submit the whole survey to the cloud and review it any time.

Features

Everything a field surveyor needs

Automatic GPS points

Every point stores latitude, longitude, time and the detected locality, with no typing.

Photo evidence

Capture up to five photos at each point with the built-in camera, stored securely online.

Surveyor questionnaire

Five quick questions per point: cleanliness, waste type, odour, road blockage and dustbin status.

Citizen interviews

Seven interview questions plus occupation, gender and age, capturing what residents see every day.

Cloud history

Submitted surveys sync to the cloud. Open Previous Surveys to review points, photos and answers.

Always up to date

New versions show up inside the app. Tap Update Now and you're on the latest release.

Built for Bengal
