Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

CivicBuddy is a field-survey app for mapping garbage hotspots. Surveyors record survey points (GPS location, photos and a short questionnaire) and short citizen interviews. This page explains what data the CivicBuddy app, this website and the CivicBuddy Backup tool collect, where it is stored, who can see it and how to contact us.

1. Data we collect

DataWhy
Google account: name, email address, profile picture To sign in surveyors and show who submitted a survey
Survey points: GPS location and accuracy, area name, date and time, photos, surveyor's answers (cleanliness, waste type, odour, etc.) The survey itself: where garbage is and what it looks like
Citizen interviews: occupation, gender, age, interview location and answers Citizens' views on local cleanliness. Citizens are not asked for their name.
Notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token To tell the phone when a new app version is available

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not collect contacts, call logs or files other than the photos taken in the app.

2. Where it is stored

  • Supabase (database and file storage): submitted surveys and photos.
  • On the phone: unfinished surveys (drafts) and surveys waiting to upload, until they are submitted or deleted.
  • Google Drive backup: copies of the survey database and photos, kept in the project maintainer's own Google Drive by the CivicBuddy Backup tool (see section 5).

3. Who can see the data

  • Signed-in app users can see submitted surveys on the in-app Survey Map (points, details and photos).
  • Surveyors (accounts that have submitted at least one survey) can download survey data from this website's Download data page.
  • Other surveyors are shown as "Surveyor 1", "Surveyor 2"… and other surveyors' email addresses are not shown in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent data, and do not share it for advertising.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Google Sign-In: signing in with a Google account.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: update notifications.
  • OpenStreetMap: map images in the Survey Map (the map server sees the phone's IP address when loading map images).
  • GitHub: app downloads and updates; Render: hosting of this website.
  • Google Drive: backups (section 5).

5. CivicBuddy Backup and Google Drive

CivicBuddy Backup is a tool used only by the project maintainer. It copies the CivicBuddy survey database and photos into a folder in the maintainer's own Google Drive, to protect the data and keep Supabase within its storage limit.

  • It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which only allows access to files the tool created itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in that Google Drive.
  • Data from Google Drive is used only to store and restore CivicBuddy backups. It is not shared, sold or used for advertising or AI training.
  • CivicBuddy Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. How long we keep data

Survey data is kept for as long as it is needed for the survey project. Surveyors can delete their own submitted surveys in the app (Previous Survey). Backups in Google Drive are kept so that data can be restored; when a survey must be removed completely, contact us and it will also be removed from the backups.

7. Children

CivicBuddy is intended for adult surveyors and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Your choices and contact

To ask what data is held about you, to correct it or to have it deleted, or for any other privacy question, please open an issue on the CivicBuddy GitHub page: github.com/Saku2025/civicBuddy/issues. Please do not include personal details in a public issue; we will reply there and arrange a private way to continue if needed.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
